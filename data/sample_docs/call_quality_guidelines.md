# Call Center Quality Assurance Guidelines

Every customer call is evaluated against four quality dimensions: greeting and identification, problem resolution, empathy and tone, and compliance. Each dimension is scored from 1 to 5 and the overall quality score is the weighted average.

Compliance carries the highest weight at 40 percent. An agent must verify the customer's identity before discussing account details, read the mandatory disclosure for any product change, and never share full card numbers. A single compliance failure caps the overall score at 2 regardless of other dimensions.

Problem resolution has a weight of 30 percent. The agent should identify the root cause, offer a solution within policy, and confirm with the customer that the issue is resolved. Calls that require a callback are marked as unresolved on first contact.

Empathy and tone has a weight of 20 percent, and greeting and identification has a weight of 10 percent. Supervisors review a random sample of five percent of calls each week plus every call that received a customer complaint.

Calls with an overall score below 3 trigger a coaching session within five working days.
