# Third-Party Vendor Risk Policy

All third-party vendors that process confidential customer data must complete a security due diligence assessment before onboarding. The assessment covers information security controls, data residency, business continuity planning and regulatory compliance.

Vendors are classified into three risk tiers. Tier 1 vendors handle customer financial data or provide critical services whose failure would interrupt operations for more than four hours. Tier 2 vendors handle internal confidential data. Tier 3 vendors have no access to confidential data.

Tier 1 vendors must be reassessed every 12 months and must provide an independent SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent certification. Tier 2 vendors are reassessed every 24 months. Tier 3 vendors are reviewed only when their contract is renewed.

Any vendor that suffers a security incident affecting our data must notify the vendor risk team within 24 hours. The vendor risk team then opens an incident record and decides whether to suspend data transfers until the root cause is understood.

Contracts with Tier 1 vendors must include a right-to-audit clause, a data breach notification clause, and an exit plan describing how data will be returned or destroyed on termination.
